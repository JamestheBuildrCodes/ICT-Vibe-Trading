# CRT-3C-v1.0 Invalidation Rules

A setup is invalid when:

- C2 does not sweep the required C1 extreme.
- C2 fails to close back inside the C1 range.
- The required aligned 15M FVG did not exist before C3 began.
- The FVG is invalidated before the required reaction/entry condition.
- The reaction occurs outside C3.
- The proposed entry requires future information.

A 5M refinement is optional; its absence alone does not invalidate a valid 15M execution.
